## Decision

Passed areas include Wave Entry ranking, strength correlation, strength stability by
fold, positive excess backtest return and sufficient trade count. Failed areas are:

- temporally stable thresholding;
- Brier skill, ECE and calibration slope;
- backtest Sharpe ratio;
- maximum drawdown.

`pre_final_gates_passed=false`, `deployment_eligible=false`, and the final holdout
remains sealed.
