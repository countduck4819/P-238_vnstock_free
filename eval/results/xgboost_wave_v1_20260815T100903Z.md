## Interpretation and status

- The classifier demonstrates useful ranking lift, especially in the top 5%, but
  overall discrimination is still moderate.
- Probability calibration greatly reduces log loss and Brier score on combined
  OOF data.
- A fixed probability threshold is not temporally stable enough for production.
  A percentile-based or regime-aware decision policy should be evaluated next.
- Strength prediction is positive overall but weak and unstable by fold; it should
  not yet be treated as a reliable magnitude forecast.
- This run is accepted as the **V1 benchmark**, not as a production acceptance.
- The final 2025+ holdout remains sealed and must not be used until the evaluation
  policy and acceptance gates are frozen.
